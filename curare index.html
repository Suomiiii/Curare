Receive the ticketFrom email, a webhook or manual input.
ProcessClean the text and extract usernames, hosts, error codes and software names.
ClassifyDetermine the type and priority, with the reasons shown.
Search the knowledge baseFind the most relevant solutions and score them.
High-confidence solution?Yes: send an automated response and close the ticket.
Otherwise assign to an IT agentThe agent sees the classification, priority and suggestions, then resolves it or escalates to the right team.

Conservative by design

Curare prefers a human over a wrong auto-close. Low-confidence tickets are routed to agents with rich context instead.

Questions

 

Does it need a lot of setup?

No. It starts with built-in rules and a small knowledge base, then improves as agents confirm or correct tickets.

Will it close tickets by mistake?

Automation is conservative: only approved articles with a high confidence score are sent automatically, and Critical and Security tickets always go to a person.

Which systems can it connect to?

The engine is designed for email, webhook and API intake, with hooks for Slack, Teams, ServiceNow, Jira and Zendesk.

Where is the demo data stored?

In your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
